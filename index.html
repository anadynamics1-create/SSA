Our Story

From a Bagalkot engineer to a team that builds the future.

  1. 01

    Roots

    Somashekhar S. Anad trained as an Electronics & Instrumentation engineer at Basaveshwar Engineering College, Bagalkot, learning how sensors, signals and control loops keep industry running.

  2. 02

    The craft

    He went deep into the full control stack: PLC logic, DCS, HMI/SCADA graphics, control panels, instrumentation and industrial protocols such as Modbus, Profibus and OPC UA.

  3. 03

    The spark

    Across North Karnataka, crop residue is often burned and cattle dung goes unused. He saw that this "waste" could become clean fuel and steady income, if the plants were engineered and controlled properly.

  4. 04

    ANADynamics

    So he built ANADynamics: one team with two specialities, automation & robotics and Bio-CNG, so clients never have to choose between a good plant and a well-controlled one.

"I started ANADynamics to build systems people can rely on every single day, and to turn the waste around us into energy and income."

Somashekhar S. AnadFounder, ANADynamics

Bio-CNG Business Opportunity

Clean energy. Recurring income.

India is building a large compressed-biogas economy. A Bio-CNG plant converts locally available organic waste into a fuel that can replace fossil CNG in vehicles and industry, while producing organic manure for farms.

With around 2+ acres of land, a reliable feedstock source and flexible investment models, landowners, farmers, dairy farms and entrepreneurs can build a long-term, sustainable business.

  • Convert organic waste into clean & green fuel
  • Generate recurring & long-term income
  • Supported by Government initiatives & subsidies*

How Bio-CNG Works

From waste to wealth in five steps.

  1. STEP 01

    Organic Waste

    Agri residue, cattle dung, food waste, sewage sludge and other biodegradable waste.

  2. STEP 02

    Anaerobic Digestion

    Waste is converted into biogas in a sealed, oxygen-free, controlled environment.

  3. STEP 03

    Biogas Upgrading

    Biogas is cleaned and upgraded to Bio-CNG quality (≥ 90% methane).

  4. STEP 04

    Bio-CNG Fuel

    Clean, green fuel for vehicles, industries and commercial applications.

  5. STEP 05

    Income Generation

    Earn consistent revenue from Bio-CNG and by-products such as organic manure.
