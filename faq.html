Frequently Asked Questions

Questions, answered.

Common questions about Bio-CNG plants, CNG and our automation services.

How much land do I need for a Bio-CNG plant?

Typically 2 acres or more, depending on plant capacity, feedstock storage and layout. We assess your land during the site visit.

What feedstock can be used?

Cattle dung, agricultural residue (such as paddy straw), food waste, industrial organic waste (such as press mud) and sewage sludge. Mixing feedstocks is common. See the feedstock page.

How much Bio-CNG will my plant produce?

It depends on the type and quantity of feedstock. Use our estimator for an indicative figure, then ask us for a proper assessment.

Are government subsidies available?

Central and State schemes support compressed biogas, subject to eligibility and government norms. See Government Support.

Do you help with documentation and applications?

Yes. We assist with documentation and the application process. Final approval rests with the respective authorities.

Who buys the Bio-CNG?

Common buyers include oil marketing companies under the SATAT initiative, CNG stations, industries, fleets and city gas networks.

What happens to the leftover slurry?

It becomes fermented organic manure (solid and liquid), which can be sold to farmers as a second income stream.

How long does digestion take?

Feedstock usually stays in the digester for several weeks, depending on the material and temperature. The plant produces gas continuously once it is running.

Is Bio-CNG the same as CNG?

In use, yes. Bio-CNG is upgraded to at least 90% methane (IS 16087) and works in the same vehicles, cylinders and stations. The difference is that it is made from waste instead of fossil gas.

Is CNG safe?

CNG is lighter than air, has a high self-ignition temperature and a narrow flammability range. Cylinders and stations are regulated by PESO. See the CNG guide.

Which automation platforms do you work with?

PLC programming in ladder and function block, DCS, HMI/SCADA design, and integration over Modbus, Profibus and OPC UA. See Automation.

Can you automate an existing plant?

Yes. We design logic, I/O mapping and panels to fit your existing equipment, or help select new hardware.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. Call us, or send an enquiry through the contact page.
