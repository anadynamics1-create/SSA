Why Invest in Bio-CNG?

Waste in. Value out.

  • Convert organic waste into clean & green fuel (CNG quality)
  • Generate recurring & long-term income
  • Reduce pollution & promote sustainable development
  • High demand for clean fuel (Bio-CNG) from vehicles and industry
  • Supported by Government initiatives & subsidies*

Instead of being burned or left to rot, crop residue, dung and food waste become a valuable feedstock. The plant produces fuel every day of the year, and the digestate becomes organic manure that improves soil health.

Revenue Streams

How a plant earns.

Bio-CNG sales

Sell compressed biogas to oil marketing company outlets under the SATAT initiative, to CNG stations, industries and vehicle fleets, or inject it into city gas networks.

Organic manure

Digestate is separated into solid fermented organic manure (FOM) and liquid manure, sold to farmers and fertiliser marketers as a second, steady income.

Waste management

Where applicable, a plant can take organic waste from dairies, food processors or local bodies, cutting their disposal costs and securing your feedstock.
