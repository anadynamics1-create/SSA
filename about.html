About ANADynamics

Automation and robotics, engineered for a greener future.

ANADynamics · Automation and Robotics is based in Bagalkot, Karnataka. We engineer control systems for industry and help landowners, farmers and entrepreneurs build Bio-CNG plants.

Our Story

Smart automation. Advanced engineering. Reliable solutions.

ANADynamics started with a simple idea: industrial systems should be designed as one whole, with logic, graphics and hardware working together from day one.

That same engineering approach now powers our second division. A Bio-CNG plant is a process plant that lives or dies on good control, so we combine automation expertise with practical support for setting up and running plants that turn waste into clean fuel.

Meet the Founder

Somashekhar S. Anad

Founder, ANADynamics · Electronics & Instrumentation Engineer

An automation engineer who works across the full control stack, from field wiring and PLC logic to DCS, operator graphics and Industrial IoT connectivity.

Somashekhar holds a B.E. in Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering from Basaveshwar Engineering College, Bagalkot (VTU).

His expertise covers PLC programming in ladder and function block, DCS engineering, HMI/SCADA design, control panel design, instrumentation and multi-protocol integration over Modbus, Profibus and OPC UA.

He founded ANADynamics to deliver automation that is designed properly, integrates cleanly and lasts, and to help bring clean Bio-CNG energy to rural Karnataka and beyond.
